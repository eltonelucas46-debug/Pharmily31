Assessment rules
  • MCQ Challenge: 30 questions, 30 marks, 15-minute target.
  • The MCQ timer turns red at 15:00. At 16:00, the assessment auto-submits and a 5-mark penalty is applied.
  • Structured Challenge: 30 structured short-answer questions, 40 minutes, 30 marks.
  • There are no essay questions. Questions may display chemical structures for identification, naming, comparison, or reaction prediction.
  • You will not be asked to draw chemical structures. Answer in words, names, formulas, or reaction products.
  • Structured answers use automated keyword rubrics for partial credit. Automated marking is an educational estimate.
  • Blank answers receive zero. Review your answers before submission.
