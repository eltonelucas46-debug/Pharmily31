Assessment rules
  • MCQ Challenge: 30 conceptual questions, 30 marks, 15-minute target (No calculations).
  • The MCQ timer turns red at 15:00. At 16:00, the assessment auto-submits and a 5-mark penalty is applied.
  • Open-Ended Calculation Challenge: 30 numerical problems in 30 minutes (30 marks). Enter only the final numerical answer (with correct units if specified).
  • Constants and unit conversion factors are provided directly within each calculation question.
  • Blank answers receive zero. Review your answers before submission.
  • Automatic marking is an educational estimate, not a substitute for lecturer marking.
