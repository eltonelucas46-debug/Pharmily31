Assessment rules
  • Open-Ended Challenge: 30 questions, 50 minutes, 30 marks.
  • All questions require an answer only. Write the final value, expression, equation, or requested result in the answer field.
  • Do not write your procedure in the answer field. The working and procedure are provided in the marking scheme after submission.
  • For numerical answers, include units where applicable. Use exact values when requested.
  • Equivalent mathematical forms may be accepted when recognised by the marking system.
  • Blank answers receive zero. Review your answers before submission.
  • Automatic marking is an educational estimate. Mathematically equivalent answers may not always be recognised automatically.
