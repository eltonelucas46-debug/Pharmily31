Assessment rules
  • MCQ Challenge: 30 questions, 30 marks, 15-minute target.
  • The MCQ timer turns red at 15:00. At 16:00, the assessment auto-submits and a 5-mark penalty is applied.
  • Open-Ended Challenge: 30 questions in 30 minutes: 25 short-answer questions and 5 essays.
  • Short answers are marked flexibly using alternative terms, synonyms, and key scientific concepts.
  • Essays receive partial credit for relevant scientific points included in the response.
  • Blank answers receive zero. Review your answers before submission.
  • Automatic marking is an educational estimate, not a substitute for lecturer marking.
